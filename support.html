Pilot — Support

Pilot is a TV tracker that works at the episode level. If something is broken, confusing, or you need help with your account, get in touch and you'll hear back.

Contact

Report content or a user

Pilot has reporting and blocking built in. On any review, list, comment, or profile, tap the ••• button and choose Report review (or Report list, Report comment, Report user), or Block user. Blocking hides that person's content from you and yours from them, in both directions.

Reported content is reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. You can also email the address above and we'll handle it the same way. Objectionable content and abusive users are removed.

Your account and your data

  • Delete your account: open Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently removes your profile, ratings, reviews, lists and watch history.
  • Notifications: the Notifications section of Settings controls what Pilot pings you about. You can also turn them off entirely in iOS Settings.
  • Privacy: see our Privacy Policy.
  • Terms: see our Terms of Service.

Common questions

Can I rate a single episode? Yes — that's the point. When you log something you choose the scope: the whole show, one season, or one episode. Each keeps its own rating and review.

Can I log something I watched a while ago? Yes. The log screen has a "Watched on" date you can set to any past day, and it lands in your diary on that date.

Where does show information come from? Show, season and episode data is provided by TMDb. This product uses the TMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDb.
